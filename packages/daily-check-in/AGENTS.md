# @line-work/daily-check-in

- Owns DailyCheckIn policy and application contract; human qualification and scope are supplied by the owning identity/workforce capability.
- A check-in is not attendance, payroll or a generic user event; do not merge its lifecycle or authorization with those owners by naming similarity.
- Preserve one-command replay behavior, current qualification/scope recheck, durable record authority and distinct unavailable/forbidden/conflict results.
- UI intent, selected organization and client timestamps are inputs only; the owner validates the authoritative subject and effective time.
